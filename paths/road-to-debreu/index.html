From preferences to utility: the road to Debreu

When a ranking can be written as a number, and when that number can be continuous

The primitive is a preference relation; a utility function is a numerical summary of it, and the question is when one exists. On finite and countable sets it always does, the lexicographic order on the unit square shows that completeness and transitivity are not enough in general, and Debreu’s theorem shows that on a connected subset of Euclidean space continuity is enough, for a utility that is itself continuous.

  1. 1

    Definition · Rubinstein, Lecture 1, p. 6

    Preference relation

    The primitive: a relation ≿, read “at least as good as”, that is reflexive, complete and transitive. Strict preference ≻ and indifference ∼ are its asymmetric and symmetric parts.

    Definition

    A binary relation ≿ on a set X, read “x≿y: x is at least as preferred as y”, is a preference relation if it is

    • reflexive: x≿x for every x∈X;
    • complete: for all distinct x,y∈X, x≿y or y≿x;
    • transitive: for all x,y,z∈X, if x≿y and y≿z, then x≿z.

  2. 2

    Definition · Rubinstein, Lecture 2, p. 12

    Utility representation

    A function U:X→R represents ≿ when x≿y⟺U(x)≥U(y) for all x and y. The rest of the path asks which preference relations have such a U.

    Definition

    Let ≿ be a preference relation on X. A function U:X→R represents ≿ if for all x,y∈X,

    x≿y⟺U(x)≥U(y).

    Such a U is a utility function for ≿, and ≿ has a utility representation if some U represents it.

  3. 3

    Claim · Rubinstein, Lecture 2, p. 13

    Utility is ordinal

    Only the ranking matters: if U represents ≿ and f:R→R is strictly increasing, then f∘U represents it too. Utility is ordinal.

    Claim

    Let ≿ be a preference relation on X, let U:X→R represent ≿, and let f:R→R be strictly increasing. Then V=f∘U also represents ≿.

    Proposition · Rubinstein, Lecture 2, p. 18

    Debreu’s theorem

    Debreu’s theorem: a continuous preference relation on a convex subset of Rn, and more generally on a connected one, has a continuous utility representation. As in the countable case, the construction starts from a countable dense set of benchmark alternatives.
