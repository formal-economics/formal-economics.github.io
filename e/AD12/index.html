Question 4

Should the young hold more stocks?

A discussion question on the advice that younger people should invest a larger share of wealth in stocks than older people.

Source
MIT 14.121 Problem Set 2, Question 4
Topic
Risk attitudes Uncertainty and risk · JEL D81
Status
Not a formal statement
Entry
AD12 Cite c
Problem Risk attitudes MIT 14.121

The problem

Standard financial advice holds that younger people should invest a higher fraction of their wealth in stocks than older people. Discuss why this might, or might not, make sense.

Not a formal statement

The question asks for a discussion of why younger investors might hold a larger share of their wealth in stocks. It contains no formal statement to check.

Intuition

One standard argument counts future labour income as a safe asset: the young hold much of their total wealth in it, so a larger share of their financial wealth can be held in stocks while the risky share of their total wealth stays the same.
