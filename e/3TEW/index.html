Problem 5 Moderately difficult

Decisive coalitions and Arrow’s theorem

The decisive-coalition route to Arrow’s theorem — field expansion, group contraction, a decisive individual — holds for distinct alternatives when there are at least three.

01The problem

Hide every answer, then reveal them one part at a time.

Decisive and almost decisive coalitions appear in several proofs of Arrow’s theorem. Fix an SWF. Call a coalition G decisive for x,y when [x≻i​y for all i∈G] forces [x≻y]; call it almost decisive for x,y when [x≻i​y for all i∈G and y≻j​x for all j∈/G] forces [x≻y]. Decisiveness for x,y implies almost decisiveness for x,y, and G is called decisive when it is decisive for every pair x,y. Assume F is an SWF meeting Par and IIA.

  1. a

    Establish the Field Expansion Lemma: when G is almost decisive for x,y, it is decisive for x,z and also for y,z, whatever z∈/{x,y} is chosen.

    1 As printed

    The field expansion lemma#

    Answer

    As printed, this does not hold in full generality: when x=y, almost decisiveness for (x,x) holds vacuously, since nobody strictly prefers x to itself. With alternatives a,b, a single individual whose preference society adopts, and G=∅, the empty coalition is almost decisive for (a,a) but not decisive for (a,b). The corrected statement requires x=y.

    Let F be a social welfare function for a finite society that satisfies Pareto and IIA, with decisive and almost decisive coalitions as defined in the problem. As printed: whenever G is almost decisive for (x,y), it is decisive for (x,z) and also for (y,z), for every z∈/{x,y}; x=y is not assumed.

    Assumptions and the Lean statement

    Intuition

    Almost decisiveness for a pair (x,x) is an empty condition, so it cannot carry information about any other pair; the lemma is meant for genuine pairs.

    2 Corrected statement

    Field expansion for distinct alternatives#

    Answer

    With x=y, the field expansion lemma holds.

    Let N be a finite society and F a social welfare function, assigning a social preference ≿ to every profile of individual orderings (≻i​), that satisfies Pareto and IIA. A coalition G⊆N is decisive for (x,y) if x≻i​y for all i∈G implies x≻y; almost decisive for (x,y) if x≻i​y for all i∈G together with y≻j​x for all j∈/G implies x≻y; and decisive if it is decisive for every pair. If x=y, z∈/{x,y} and G is almost decisive for (x,y), then G is decisive for (x,z) and for (y,z).

    The assumptions of the statement, each beside the line of the Lean statement that states it
    Number As the problem states it Correspondence In Lean

    2 F satisfies Pareto: if every individual prefers x to y, so does society. (hPar : Par F) Matches the source

    6 G is almost decisive for (x,y). (h : AlmostDecisiveWrt F G x y) Matches the source

    Each assumption is stated by the line of Lean beside it. Point at a row to find its line in the full statement below. The added condition is the one the printed statement lacks.

    The definitions it is stated with

    Intuition

    Given a profile in which G prefers x to z, rearrange y so that G ranks x≻y≻z and everyone else ranks y above both. Almost decisiveness gives x≻y, Pareto gives y≻z, so x≻z, and IIA carries the verdict back to the original profile. For the pair (y,z), put y above x for everyone and x above z within G: Pareto gives y≻x, the first half gives x≻z, so y≻z.

  2. b

    Prove that a coalition G almost decisive for x,y is decisive.

    1 As printed

    Almost decisive for one pair implies decisive#

    2 Corrected statement

    Almost decisive for a pair implies decisive, given three alternatives#

  3. c

    Establish the Group Contraction Lemma: when G is decisive with ∣G∣≥2, there is a decisive G′⊂G.

    1 As printed

    The group contraction lemma#

    2 Corrected statement

    Prove that {i∗} is decisive for some individual i∗.

    1 As printed

    Some individual is decisive#

    2 Corrected statement

03Related notionsWhat implies what →

  • DDefines Coalition decisive with respect to an ordered pair
  • DDefines Coalition almost decisive with respect to an ordered pair
  • DDefines Decisive coalition
  • ⇒Shows Coalition decisive with respect to an ordered pair implies Coalition almost decisive with respect to an ordered pair (finitely many individuals)
  • ⇒Shows Coalition almost decisive with respect to an ordered pair and Pareto condition (Par) and Independence of irrelevant alternatives (IIA) implies Coalition decisive with respect to an ordered pair (finitely many individuals; x≠yx \ne yx=y (added); a third alternative zzz; the conclusion is for (x,z)(x,z)(x,z) and (y,z)(y,z)(y,z))
  • ⇒Shows Coalition almost decisive with respect to an ordered pair and Pareto condition (Par) and Independence of irrelevant alternatives (IIA) implies Decisive coalition (finitely many individuals; x≠yx \ne yx=y and at least three alternatives (added))
  • ⇒Shows Pareto condition (Par) and Independence of irrelevant alternatives (IIA) implies Decisive coalition (finitely many individuals; at least three alternatives (added); the decisive coalition is a singleton)
  • ⇒Shows Decisive coalition and Pareto condition (Par) and Independence of irrelevant alternatives (IIA) implies Decisive coalition (finitely many individuals; at least three alternatives (added); the coalition has at least two members)
