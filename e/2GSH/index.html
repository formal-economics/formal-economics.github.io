The demand correspondence

The set of all optimal bundles, computed for perfect substitutes and shown to be upper semi-continuous for continuous preferences.

(a)

Demand correspondence for perfect substitutes

With perfect substitutes, all wealth goes to a cheapest good, and with equal prices every budget-exhausting bundle is optimal.

Machine-checked

Answer · in our words

Calculated as displayed: all wealth goes to a cheapest good, and when prices are equal every budget-exhausting bundle is optimal. This description of X(p,w)X(p, w)X(p,w) is our answer to the calculation the book asks for.

Intuition

The goods are perfect substitutes, so the consumer buys the most total quantity she can afford, which means spending everything on whichever good is cheaper.

(b)

Continuous preferences give an upper semi-continuous demand

If preferences are continuous, limits of optimal bundles are optimal at the limiting prices and wealth, with no convexity needed.

Machine-checked

Let ≿\succsim≿ on R+K\mathbb{R}^K_+R+K​ be continuous, not necessarily convex. Then the demand correspondence X(p,w)X(p, w)X(p,w) is upper semi-continuous on R++K+1\mathbb{R}^{K+1}_{++}R++K+1​: if (pn,wn)→(p,w){(p^n, w^n) \to (p, w)}(pn,wn)→(p,w) with all terms and the limit in R++K+1\mathbb{R}^{K+1}_{++}R++K+1​, and xn∈X(pn,wn){x^n \in X(p^n, w^n)}xn∈X(pn,wn) converge to xxx, then x∈X(p,w){x \in X(p, w)}x∈X(p,w).

Assumptions

  • ≿\succsim≿ is a continuous preference relation on R+K\mathbb{R}^K_+R+K​. Matches the source
Technical conditions (1)needed only to state it formally
  • Upper semi-continuity is defined for correspondences between coordinate spaces Rm\mathbb{R}^mRm and Rn\mathbb{R}^nRn, with convergence in the Euclidean distance.

Answer

Proved.

Intuition

Any bundle affordable at the limit can be approached by bundles affordable along the sequence, and continuity of preferences carries each comparison ”xnx^nxn is at least as good” to the limit: a limit of optimal choices is optimal.
