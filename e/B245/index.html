How deadweight loss changes with the tax

The derivatives in the tax of two deadweight-loss measures: both are zero at t = 0; for t > 0 the measure at the old utility rises, while the one at the new utility can fall.

Corrected Consumer demand and duality D11

As printed, this statement does not hold in full generality; a corrected companion is proved.
