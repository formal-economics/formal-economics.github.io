Definition

Pareto condition (Par)

Unanimous strict preferences are respected by society.

Rubinstein, Lecture 9: Social Choice, p. 130 Social choice · JEL D71, D72 Entry CE9N Citec

01Statement

Definition. An SWF F satisfies Par if for all x,y∈X and every profile (≻i​)i∈N​: if x≻i​y for every i, then x≻y, where ≻ is the strict part of the social preference F(≻1​,…,≻n​).

Intuition
