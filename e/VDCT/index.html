Convex technologies and concave production functions

When a technology and a production function describe the same single-output producer, the technology is convex exactly when the production function is concave.

Machine-checked Producers and the firm D21D24 Easy
Problem Producers and the firm Rubinstein (2012)

Answer

Shown, in both directions: Z{Z}Z is convex if and only if f{f}f is concave.

Intuition

Mixing two feasible plans of a convex technology is feasible, so the mixture of two input vectors can produce at least the mixture of their outputs: that is concavity of f.{f}.f. Conversely, when f{f}f is concave, the plans on or below its graph, with inputs freely disposable, form a convex set.
