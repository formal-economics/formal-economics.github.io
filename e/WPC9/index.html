Definition

Weak axiom of revealed preference (choice correspondences)

Once x is revealed at least as good as y, y is never revealed strictly better than x.

Rubinstein, Lecture 3: Choice, p. 32 Choice and rationalizability · JEL D01, D11 Entry WPC9 Citec

01Statement

Definition. A choice correspondence C for a context (X,D) satisfies the weak axiom (WA) if for all A,B∈D and all x,y∈A∩B: if x∈C(A) and y∈C(B), then x∈C(B).

Intuition

Once x has been revealed at least as good as y, y cannot be revealed strictly better than x: whenever y is chosen with x available, so is x.
