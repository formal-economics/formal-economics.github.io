Definition

Hicksian demand and the expenditure function

The cheapest bundle at least as good as a target bundle, and its cost.

Rubinstein, Lecture 6: More Economic Agents, p. 87 Consumer demand and duality · JEL D11 Entry 2RVJ Citec

01Statement

Definition. Let ≿ be a preference relation on X=R+K​, p a price vector and z∈X a target bundle. The dual problem D(p,z) is

xmin​{p⋅x:x≿z}.

When it has a unique solution, that solution is the Hicksian demand h(p,z), and e(p,z)=p⋅h(p,z) is the expenditure function.

Intuition

The dual consumer fixes a target level of satisfaction, that of the bundle z, and looks for the cheapest way to reach it.
