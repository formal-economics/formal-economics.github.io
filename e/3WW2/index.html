Quasi-linearity in commodity 1

In the 2025 printing, the sentence defining quasi-linearity in commodity 1 ends before the range of ε is stated.

As printed

As printed (2025 edition): a preference relation ≿\succsim≿ on R+K{\mathbb{R}^K_+}R+K​ is quasi-linear in commodity 1 if x≿y{x \succsim y}x≿y implies (x+εe1)≿(y+εe1)(x + \varepsilon e_1) \succsim (y + \varepsilon e_1)(x+εe1​)≿(y+εe1​), where e1=(1,0,…,0){e_1 = (1, 0, \dots, 0)}e1​=(1,0,…,0) and ε\varepsilonε. The sentence ends there, so the range of ε\varepsilonε is not stated. The earlier 2020 printing completes it: “ε\varepsilonε is a real number”.

Intuition

Without a range for ε\varepsilonε the sentence does not determine a property; the machine-checked definition uses the range given in the 2020 printing.

Quasi-linear preferences (in commodity 1)

A preference is quasi-linear in good 1 if adding the same amount of good 1 to two bundles never reverses their comparison.

Machine-checked

A preference relation ≿\succsim≿ on X=R+K{X = \mathbb{R}^K_+}X=R+K​ is quasi-linear in commodity 1 if for all bundles x,y{x, y}x,y and every real ε\varepsilonε for which x+εe1{x + \varepsilon e_1}x+εe1​ and y+εe1{y + \varepsilon e_1}y+εe1​ are bundles,

x≿y  ⟹  x+εe1≿y+εe1,x \succsim y \implies x + \varepsilon e_1 \succsim y + \varepsilon e_1,x≿y⟹x+εe1​≿y+εe1​,

where e1=(1,0,…,0){e_1 = (1, 0, \dots, 0)}e1​=(1,0,…,0).

Assumptions

  • The commodity space is X=R+K{X = \mathbb{R}^K_+}X=R+K​, bundles of KKK goods. Matches the source
  • ≿\succsim≿ is a preference relation on XXX. Matches the source
  • ε\varepsilonε ranges over all real numbers, positive or negative (the range of the 2020 printing). Matches the source
Technical conditions (1)needed only to state it formally
  • The shifted bundles x+εe1{x + \varepsilon e_1}x+εe1​ and y+εe1{y + \varepsilon e_1}y+εe1​ must lie in R+K{\mathbb{R}^K_+}R+K​, so that the comparison is defined.

Intuition

Commodity 1 acts like money: adding or removing the same amount of it leaves comparisons unchanged, so indifference curves are translates of one another along the axis of good 1.
