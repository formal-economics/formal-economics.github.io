Monotonicity lemma for the independence axiom

Under independence, shifting probability toward the better prize is strictly better.

Intuition

Independence says that mixing two lotteries with a common third lottery preserves their ranking. Writing the lottery with more weight on xxx as a mixture of the other lottery with [x][x][x] shows that it is strictly better.
