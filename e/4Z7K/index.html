Monotonicity lemma for the independence axiom

Under independence, shifting probability toward the better prize is strictly better.

Rubinstein, Lecture 7: Expected Utility, p. 102 Choice under uncertainty · JEL D81 Entry 4Z7K Citec

01Statement

Lemma. Let Z be a finite set of prizes and ≿ a preference relation on the set L(Z) of lotteries over Z that satisfies the independence axiom. If x,y∈Z and [x]≻[y], then for all 1≥α>β≥0,

αx⊕(1−α)y≻βx⊕(1−β)y.

The definitions it is stated with

Intuition

Independence says that mixing two lotteries with a common third lottery preserves their ranking. Writing the lottery with more weight on x as a mixture of the other lottery with [x] shows that it is strictly better.
