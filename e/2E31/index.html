Claim

Arrow’s impossibility theorem

With three or more alternatives, Pareto and IIA leave only dictatorial social welfare functions.

Result Social choice Rubinstein

01Statement

Claim. Let N be a finite set of individuals and let X contain at least three alternatives. If a social welfare function F satisfies Par and IIA, then F is dictatorial: there is an individual i∗ such that for every profile and all x,y∈X, society strictly prefers x to y if and only if x≻i∗​y.

The definition it is stated with

Intuition

Pareto and IIA let decisive power over one pair of alternatives spread to every pair; with three or more alternatives, transitivity of the social ranking forces that power into the hands of a single individual.
