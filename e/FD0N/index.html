Lemma Rubinstein (2012), Lecture 2, p. 15

FD0N

Lexicographic preferences are preference relations

The lexicographic preference induced by finitely many preference relations is complete and transitive.

Machine-checked Utility representation D11D01
Result Utility representation Rubinstein (2012)

Lemma

Let ≿1,…,≿K\succsim_1, \dots, \succsim_K≿1​,…,≿K​ be preference relations on XXX. The lexicographic preference ≿L\succsim_L≿L​ they induce is a preference relation on XXX.

Assumptions

  • ≿1,…,≿K\succsim_1, \dots, \succsim_K≿1​,…,≿K​ is a finite list of preference relations on XXX. Matches the source

Conclusion

≿L\succsim_L≿L​ is reflexive, complete and transitive.

Intuition

Two alternatives are either indifferent under every criterion, or there is a first criterion that separates them, and that criterion settles the comparison. In a chain of two comparisons, the earlier of the two deciding criteria decides the combined one.
