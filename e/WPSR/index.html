What utility maximization implies for two demand functions

Given demand formulas for two of three goods, Walras’ law yields the third, both formulas are homogeneous of degree zero, and utility maximization forces α = 100, β = γ = −5.

With locally nonsatiated preferences, the demand for good 3 is the wealth left after buying the given amounts of goods 1 and 2, divided by p₃.

Both demand formulas are homogeneous of degree zero

Scaling all three prices and wealth by the same positive factor leaves both demand formulas unchanged.

Yes: both demand functions are homogeneous of degree zero in prices and wealth, as Walrasian demand must be.

Utility maximization forces α = 100 and β = γ = −5

Some utility function generates the two demand formulas on an open set of prices and wealth exactly when α = 100, β = −5 and γ = −5; δ is left free.
