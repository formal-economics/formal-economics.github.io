Representing “approximately the same”

Six axioms for a similarity relation on the unit interval reduce it to closeness within a fixed tolerance after a continuous, increasing rescaling.

Field

01The problem

Hide every answer, then reveal them one part at a time.

On X=[0,1] consider properties of a relation S read “approximately the same”: (S-1) aSa; (S-2) aSb⇒bSa; (S-3) the graph of S is closed; (S-4) betweenness: if d≥c≥b≥a and dSa, then cSb; (S-5) every a has an open interval around it whose points x satisfy xSa; (S-6) M(a)=max{x:xSa} and m(a)=min{x:aSx} are weakly increasing, and strictly increasing where they do not take the values 0 or 1.

Machine-checked statement

Answer

(b) Proved, including that M and m are well defined for Sε​. (c) Proved: for every ε>0 such an H exists. Part (a), an invitation to discuss the axioms, is not formalized.

For a relation S on X=[0,1] consider: (S-1) aSa; (S-2) aSb⇒bSa; (S-3) the graph of S is closed in X×X; (S-4) if d≥c≥b≥a and dSa, then cSb; (S-5) each a has an open interval around it such that xSa for every x∈X in the interval; (S-6) M(a)=max{x:xSa} and m(a)=min{x:aSx} exist, are weakly increasing, and f(a)<f(b) for f=M,m whenever a<b and neither f(a) nor f(b) equals 0 or 1.

  • (b) For every ε>0, the relation aSε​b⟺∣b−a∣≤ε satisfies (S-1)–(S-6).
  • (c) If S satisfies (S-1)–(S-6) and ε>0, there is a strictly increasing, continuous H:[0,1]→R with aSb⟺∣H(a)−H(b)∣≤ε for all a,b.

Intuition

Part (c) is to “approximately the same” what a utility function is to a preference: after a suitable monotone change of scale, every such relation is just closeness within a fixed tolerance. Property (S-6) makes the tolerance bands slide monotonically, which is what lets a single rescaling work.
