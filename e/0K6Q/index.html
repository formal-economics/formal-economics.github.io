Claim

Satisficing is rationalizable

Satisficing choices can always be explained as maximization of some preference relation.

Rubinstein, Lecture 3: Choice, p. 30 Choice and rationalizability · JEL D01, D11 Entry 0K6Q Citec

01Statement

Claim. Let (X,D) be a context, v a valuation, v∗ a threshold and O an ordering of X such that every A∈D has a unique satisficing choice. Then Cv,v∗,O​ can be rationalized: there is a preference relation ≿ on X such that every A∈D has a unique best element under ≿ and Cv,v∗,O​(A)=C≿​(A) for all A∈D.

Intuition

Rank every satisfactory alternative above every unsatisfactory one, order the satisfactory alternatives by O and the unsatisfactory ones by the reverse of O. Maximizing this preference reproduces satisficing exactly.
