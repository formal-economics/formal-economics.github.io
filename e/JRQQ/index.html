Luce's choice axiom yields the ratio representation

If choice probabilities factor through every sub-menu, each alternative has a positive weight and is chosen with probability proportional to it.

Corrected statement

Answer

(a) Proved: PI is a genuine restriction. (b) Proved: PI implies the Luce representation. Uniqueness of vvv and the converse implication are not part of the statement.

Intuition

PI says the odds of choosing aaa over bbb do not depend on what else is on the menu. Fix a reference alternative, define each alternative’s weight by its odds against it, and the ratio formula follows.

Answer

Proved: the two requested examples exist. Whether they are “reasonable”, as the book asks, is a matter of interpretation and is not formalized.

Intuition

Similarity effects violate PI: adding a near-duplicate of aaa takes probability mostly from aaa rather than proportionally from every alternative.
