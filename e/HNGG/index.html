Definition

Independence of irrelevant alternatives (IIA)

The social ranking of two alternatives depends only on individuals’ rankings of those two.

Rubinstein, Lecture 9: Social Choice, pp. 130–131 Social choice · JEL D71, D72 Entry HNGG Citec

Defined in Lean, as printed · 11 July 2026

01Statement

Definition. An SWF F satisfies IIA if for all x,y∈X and any two profiles P=(≻i​) and P′=(⊳i​): if x≻i​y⟺x⊳i​y for every i, then x≿y⟺x⊵y, where ≿=F(P) and ⊵=F(P′).

Intuition

Information about third alternatives, or about how strongly individuals feel, cannot affect how society compares x and y.
