Quasi-linear preferences and the numeraire

Under continuity and monotonicity, with quasi-linearity and strong monotonicity in good 1, every bundle can be priced in units of good 1.

Machine-checked Consumer preferences D11 Difficult
Problem Consumer preferences Rubinstein (2012)

The question

Answer

Proved for every KKK (existence of v(x)v(x)v(x); uniqueness is not part of the statement).

Intuition

Slide along the axis of good 1: the origin is no better than xxx, and enough of good 1 alone is better than xxx, so by continuity some point in between is exactly as good. Good 1 acts as money, and v(x)v(x)v(x) is the value of xxx measured in it.
