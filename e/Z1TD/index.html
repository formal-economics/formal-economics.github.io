Continuity of the Hicksian demand

Monotone, continuous and strictly convex preferences make the Hicksian demand h(p, z) continuous in prices and in the reference bundle.

01The problem

Hide every answer, then reveal them one part at a time.

Show that the Hicksian demand function h(p,z) is continuous when preferences are monotonic, continuous and strictly convex.

Machine-checked statement

Answer

Shown: under these three conditions the solution of the dual problem depends continuously on (p,z), jointly in prices and the reference bundle.

Let ≿ be a preference on X=R+K​ that is monotone, continuous and strictly convex. For prices p≫0 and a bundle z∈X, the dual problem D(p,z) is minx​{p⋅x:x≿z}, and its solution is the Hicksian demand h(p,z). Then h is continuous: for every p≫0, z∈X, every solution x of D(p,z) and every ε>0 there is δ>0 such that every solution x′ of D(p′,z′) with p′≫0 satisfies ∥x′−x∥<ε whenever ​(p′,z′)−(p,z)​<δ.

1 ≿ is monotone: x≥y implies x≿y, and x≫y implies x≻y. (hmono : pref.IsMonotone) Matches the source
2 ≿ is continuous: if a≻b, then x≻y for all x near a and y near b. (hcont : pref.IsContinuous) Matches the source
3 ≿ is strictly convex: if a≿y, b≿y and a=b, then λa+(1−λ)b≻y for every λ∈(0,1). (hstrict : pref.IsStrictlyConvex) Matches the source

Intuition

Strict convexity leaves a single cheapest bundle in the set of bundles at least as good as z. Continuity and monotonicity keep that set, and the least cost of reaching it, from jumping as (p,z) moves, so the unique cheapest bundle cannot jump either.
