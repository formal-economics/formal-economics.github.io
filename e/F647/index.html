Claim

The technology derived from a production function

An increasing, continuous, concave production function with f(0) = 0 yields a technology satisfying the standard conditions.

Rubinstein, Lecture 6: More Economic Agents, p. 89 Producers and the firm · JEL D21, D24 Entry F647 Citec

01Statement

Claim. Let f be a real-valued function on R+K−1​ that is increasing (nondecreasing), continuous and concave, with f(0)=0. Then the derived technology Z(f) satisfies all five conditions: 0∈Z(f); Z(f)∩R+K​⊆{0}; free disposal; Z(f) is closed; Z(f) is convex.

Technical conditions needed only to state it formally
  • The commodities are indexed so that the last one is the output.
  • Concavity is stated on R+K−1​, whose convexity is automatic.

Conclusion

Z(f) satisfies conditions 1 to 5.

Intuition

Since f(0)=0, inaction is feasible and nothing can be produced without inputs; free disposal is built into Z(f), continuity of f makes Z(f) closed, and concavity of f makes it convex.
