Definition

Technology

Production possibilities described by a set of feasible plans, with the standard conditions.

Rubinstein, Lecture 6: More Economic Agents, pp. 88–89 Producers and the firm · JEL D21, D24 Entry DD0P Citec

01Statement

Definition. A technology is a set Z⊆RK of production combinations; positive components are outputs and negative components inputs. Conditions that may be imposed:

  1. 0∈Z: inaction is possible;
  2. no production without resources: no z∈Z∩R+K​ other than 0;
  3. free disposal: z∈Z and z′≤z imply z′∈Z;
  4. Z is closed;
  5. Z is convex.

If Z is convex and 0∈Z, then λz∈Z for every z∈Z and every λ∈[0,1).

Conclusion

If Z is convex and 0∈Z, then λz∈Z for all z∈Z and λ∈[0,1).

The definition it is stated with

Intuition

Convexity together with inaction gives non-increasing returns to scale: every feasible plan can be scaled down.
