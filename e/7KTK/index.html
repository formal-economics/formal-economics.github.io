Properties of the supply function

Homogeneity, continuity and the law of supply.

Rubinstein, Lecture 6: More Economic Agents, p. 92 Producers and the firm · JEL D21, D24 Entry 7KTK Citec

01Statement

Claim. Let Z⊆RK be a technology. Each statement assumes that the producer’s problems it mentions have unique solutions.

  1. z(λp)=z(p) for every λ>0.
  2. If Z is closed, convex and bounded above (there is B with zk​≤B for all z∈Z and all k), then z is continuous wherever it is defined.
  3. (Law of supply.) If z(p)=z(p′), then (p−p′)⋅(z(p)−z(p′))>0. In particular, if p and p′ differ only in coordinate k, with pk​>pk′​, and z(p)=z(p′), then zk​(p)>zk​(p′).

Conclusion

Properties 1 to 3.

Intuition

Each of z(p) and z(p′) is the unique profit maximizer at its own prices, so each earns strictly more than the other at those prices; adding the two inequalities gives the law of supply.
