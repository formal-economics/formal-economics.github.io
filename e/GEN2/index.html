Proposition

Rationalizability of choice functions satisfying condition α

If the domain contains every menu of at most three alternatives, every choice function satisfying condition α is rationalizable.

Rubinstein, Lecture 3: Choice, p. 28 Choice and rationalizability · JEL D01, D11 Entry GEN2 Citec

01Statement

Proposition. Let C be a choice function for a context (X,D) in which D contains every nonempty subset of X with at most three elements. If C satisfies condition α, then C can be rationalized: there is a preference relation ≿ on X with C=C≿​ on D.

Intuition

Let x≿y mean that x is chosen from {x,y}. Condition α on triples makes this relation transitive, and condition α on larger problems shows that C(A) beats every other member of A in pairwise choice.
