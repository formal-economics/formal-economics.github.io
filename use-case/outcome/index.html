Outcome

All 14 numbered results of the paper are proved.

Success: all 14 numbered results of the paper are proved.

Its definitions (17 passages) and its assumption are stated in Lean, and each statement is checked against the paper. In Lean: 20 theorems, 37 definitions and 74 helper lemmas.

In four of them, Lemma 1, Proposition 2, Proposition 3 and Proposition 6, the Lean statement writes out a condition that the paper gives beside the result or in its proof: their check shows where.
