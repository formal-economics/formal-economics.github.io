Arrow’s impossibility theorem

Rubinstein, Lecture 9, p. 131

As printed

Let N be a finite set of individuals and let X contain at least three alternatives. If a social welfare function F satisfies Par and IIA, then F is dictatorial: there is an individual i∗ such that for every profile and all x,y∈X, society strictly prefers x to y if and only if x≻i∗​y.

Expected utility theorem

Rubinstein, Lecture 7, p. 104

As printed

Let Z be a finite set of prizes and ≿ a preference relation on L(Z) that satisfies the independence axiom and the continuity axiom. Then there are numbers (v(z))z∈Z​ such that the expected value

U(p)=z∈Z∑​p(z)v(z)

represents ≿: for all p,q∈L(Z), p≿q if and only if U(p)≥U(q).

In Lean

Roy’s identity

Rubinstein, Lecture 6, p. 85

As printed

Let ≿ be a monotone preference relation on X=R+K​, let x(p,w) be a best bundle of B(p,w) under ≿ for every p≫0 and w>0, and fix p∗≫0 and w∗>0.

  1. The point (p∗,w∗) lies on H={(p,w):p⋅x(p∗,w∗)=w}, and every (p,w)∈H with p≫0, w>0 satisfies (p,w)≿∗(p∗,w∗).
  2. (Roy’s identity.) If ≿∗ is represented by a differentiable indirect utility function v(p,w), then for every k:
−∂v/∂w(p∗,w∗)∂v/∂pk​(p∗,w∗)​=xk​(p∗,w∗).

Slutsky’s compensated law of demand

Rubinstein, Lecture 5, p. 78

As printed

Let x be a demand function (with x(p,w)∈B(p,w), homogeneous of degree zero) that satisfies Walras’ law and the weak axiom of revealed preference. Let p≫0 and w>0, let p′≫0, and let w′=p′⋅x(p,w) be the compensated wealth. Then either x(p′,w′)=x(p,w), or

(p′−p)⋅(x(p′,w′)−x(p,w))<0.

How every entry is checked

Stated as printed, proved by machine, with nothing added in between.

  1. As printed

    Each statement is written out exactly as its source prints it, with the page, so you can hold it against the book.

  2. In Lean

    The same statement is written in the Lean proof assistant. Each assumption of the source is matched to one line of Lean, and nothing is added.

  3. Checked

    Lean verifies every step of the proof. Where a statement needs an added condition, its counterexample is checked too, beside the version with that condition.
